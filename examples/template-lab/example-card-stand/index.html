Paper-card slot stand · 80 × 28 × 12 mm · cadquery

Inspect your result

Broad base down, slot upward. For lightweight paper/photo cards on a desk only, not phones, tablets or structural loads. The vertical slot has a closed base; measure card thickness and allow clearance. Holding stability and fit are not physically verified.
